# Research Notes

What led to the segments, prices, and positioning on cybershare.dev. Compiled during the initial build (Feb 2026, updated as facts change). Sources are public brand sites and Google search results.

## Idea

A design/dev shop that builds simple, phone-first websites for local businesses: restaurants, auto shops, and every other Main Street business. Fixed price, you own everything.

## Competitor scan — how similar agencies position themselves

Studied three comparable "cheap fixed-price site" offers to calibrate our promise and pricing.

### FactoryJet (factoryjet.com) — "web design factory"
- **Model:** Flat-rate site packages, fast delivery, US-based support.
- **For us:** validated the flat-rate, "same price no matter the business" format, and the speed sell ("live within 14 days"). They sell on process and delivery time; we undersell them on ownership (we give the files) and personalization (we scope on a call).

### DirectOrders (directorders.com) — restaurant-specific sites
- **Model:** restaurant sites with menus, ordering buttons, review displays; monthly SaaS pricing.
- **For us:** confirmed that restaurants are the #1 local search vertical (Google: restaurant queries convert for "near me" intent). They charge *recurring*; we price one-time with cheap add-ons, which small owners read as low-risk.

### LuperIQ (luperiq.com) — SMB web packages
- **Model:** tiered packages for SMBs, SEO add-ons.
- **For us:** validated 3-visual-tier pricing (our Start/Grow/Own) and the add-ons model (ours are honest line items, not upsell funnels).

## Segment selection

Primary segments decided by search behavior + money spent on local ads:

1. **Restaurants & cafés** — highest "near me" search volume; menu, hours, order = one-tap conversions.
2. **Auto shops & garages** — service-page sites that drive appointment calls; ASE cert trust.
3. **Every other local business** — salons, gyms, dentists, landscapers, boutiques, food trucks (the "from $395" tier).

## Pricing evidence

- DIY builders (Wix/Squarespace): $23-$40/mo with hidden add-ons — small owners complain about cost + lock-in.
- Our tiers: $395 / $1,100 / $1,400 one-time + hosting/edits $35/mo optional. Cheaper than a designer retainer, more personal than a template.
- "Most chosen" middle tier is the psychological default; the $599 "from" for restaurant/auto reflects more pages vs the flat $395.

## SEO claims we allow ourselves

We bill the Included section with delivery-focused promises (map, click-to-call, sitemap, analytics access), never with rankings or "we'll get you #1" language. The site itself follows this (no meta keyword claims).
